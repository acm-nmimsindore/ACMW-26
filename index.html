About Our Chapter

EMPOWERING
WOMEN IN
COMPUTING

ACM-W NMIMS Indore is the official women in computing chapter of ACM — creating an inclusive environment where aspiring technologists learn, lead, and grow together.

Through workshops, mentorship, technical events, and leadership opportunities, we empower students to excel in computing and leave a lasting impact.
